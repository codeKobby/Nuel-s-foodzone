# Public Redesign V3 Direction

## User feedback translated into design decisions

The previous version missed on three visible dimensions: the typography felt too stylized and editorial for the brand, the sharp corners made the site feel rigid and artificial, and the hero did not create a convincing first impression. This revision should feel like a confident modern food brand rather than a design portfolio experiment.

## Typography

Use a friendly, highly legible sans-serif family for the entire public experience. Reserve weight and scale—not an ornamental display face—for hierarchy. Headlines should feel warm and confident, with sentence case instead of forced line breaks or theatrical oversized words. Body copy should be comfortable to scan on mobile. Avoid mixing a fashion/editorial serif with a geometric sans.

## Corner language

Use a consistent medium radius around 16–20px for image panels, menu cards, buttons, and form surfaces. Avoid both extremes: no sharp square controls everywhere, and no pill-shaped buttons or excessive rounded containers. Buttons can use an 8–10px radius; cards can use 18px; large hero/image panels can use 24px when the composition benefits from it.

## Hero direction

The hero should be a strong, practical restaurant landing moment: a full-width or wide split composition with one clear food focal point, a readable headline, a short proposition, and two intentional actions. Use a carefully cropped food image with a quiet text area or an anchored color panel. Avoid putting text over busy food, avoid multiple competing headlines, and avoid stacking decorative labels. The first viewport should communicate what Nuel’s Foodzone is, what Dinner is, and how to order.

## Overall visual system

Use warm cream, deep brown/charcoal, tomato red, and a muted green accent. Use soft borders, restrained shadows, intentional whitespace, and fewer competing modules. Keep the ordering page practical and denser than the home page, but share the same typography, radius, and color tokens. The public site should feel approachable, contemporary, Ghanaian, and ready for real customers—not like a generic AI-generated restaurant template.
